# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds. Focus discussion on the work and help others reproduce technical problems.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, student data, private screenshots, or other sensitive material when requesting help.

## Reporting and moderation

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For abuse or private conduct concerns, use GitHub's Report abuse tools; do not publish private details in an issue. Maintainers may arrange a private conversation when an appropriate channel is available.

The repository maintainer may request edits, remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, or restrict participation using repository permissions. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and protect participants' privacy. This policy applies to interactions in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.
